# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define the quality strategy, validate acceptance criteria, and ensure releases meet the agreed level of confidence before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans during project planning
- Translate acceptance criteria into executable test cases
- Coordinate automated and manual quality validation
- Track defects, quality signals, and release readiness
- Partner with Developers and Product Managers to confirm feature completeness

### Goals
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Improve confidence in every release milestone
- Align testing with delivery goals and customer impact

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality dashboards
- Defect triage and release-readiness reviews
- Daily standups and pre-release verification meetings

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate implementation and resolve defect risk
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release quality expectations
- Reports readiness and blockers to Project Managers for scheduling and escalation

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, decision authority, and sponsorship for the project. They help prioritize work and ensure outcomes remain aligned with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project goals, milestones, and funding decisions
- Provide business priorities, customer context, and trade-off guidance
- Review major milestones and strategic direction
- Support escalation and issue resolution when needed
- Ensure alignment between delivery teams and business objectives

### Goals
- Maintain strategic alignment and sponsorship
- Enable timely, informed decisions
- Support value realization from the project

### Typical Communication
- Steering reviews and milestone updates
- Executive briefings and status summaries
- High-level decision and escalation conversations

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides direction to Product Managers on strategy and priority
- Works with Project Managers on scope, risk, and delivery commitments
- Reviews outcomes with Developers and cross-functional teams to confirm business value

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads maintain the project’s security posture and validate that controls, policies, and release practices meet required standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, dependencies, and security risks during planning and execution
- Validate security scanning, vulnerability remediation, and compliance requirements
- Define incident response expectations and escalation paths
- Support release gating for critical security and policy controls
- Partner with cross-functional teams on secure design and deployment practices

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Embed secure delivery practices into the project lifecycle
- Ensure trustworthy and auditable releases

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and risk assessments
- Vulnerability reports and mitigation updates
- Incident updates and sign-off checkpoints

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers on secure coding practices and remediation
- Coordinates with Project Managers on risk, mitigation, and release timing
- Provides guidance to Product Managers and Sponsors on policy constraints and business impact

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps / Release Engineers support reliable delivery by managing CI/CD pipelines, deployment workflows, and operational readiness for each release.

### Responsibilities
- Configure and maintain build, test, and deployment automation
- Prepare deployment runbooks, rollback plans, and operational checks
- Support staging and production release execution
- Monitor release health and production signals after deployment
- Partner with Engineering and QA to reduce release risk and deployment friction

### Goals
- Increase release reliability and repeatability
- Shorten cycle time without sacrificing safety
- Improve observability and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Deployment readiness reviews and rollback planning
- Pipeline health, release dashboards, and incident updates
- Cross-team coordination around production changes

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Developers to automate and validate deployment workflows
- Coordinates with QA / Testing Leads on release checks and smoke tests
- Works with Project Managers to align release timing and communication

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches help the team work effectively, remove delivery friction, and keep Agile practices aligned with team needs and project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Coach teams on agile practices, clear ownership, and effective collaboration
- Help identify blockers and escalate process issues early
- Track team health metrics such as flow, velocity, and improvement opportunities
- Support continuous improvement and consistent execution rhythm

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and delivery flow
- Create a healthy, transparent, and high-performing environment
- Help the team deliver predictable, high-quality outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Agile ceremonies and team coaching sessions
- Retrospective notes and improvement actions
- Team health updates and process recommendations

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Project Managers with meeting facilitation and delivery visibility
- Helps Developers and Product Managers clarify scope, priorities, and collaboration practices
- Brings issues to Sponsors and stakeholders when process or dependency gaps need broader attention

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify how accountability, quality, security, delivery operations, and stakeholder coordination fit into the broader project management lifecycle.
